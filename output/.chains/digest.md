*Digest — 2026-09-09*

_TL;DR: DeepSeek's two-day V4.1 Flash test window closes tomorrow, Qualcomm locked a $60B multi-generation AI chip deal with Amazon, and OpenAI's Navier-Stokes solve is now a credit-attribution fight with an Anthropic researcher._

1. *DeepSeek quietly tests V4.1 Flash — window closes September 10*
   DeepSeek opened a public test endpoint (`deepseek-v4.1-flash-expires-on-0910`) claiming it beats V4 Pro on performance, cost, and speed with native multimodal support; all V4 Pro traffic will route to it once official.
   Why it matters: a free two-day window to benchmark a frontier-tier model before the official launch and pricing land.
   https://cellcog.ai/blog/deepseek-v4-1-flash-release-date/

2. *Qualcomm and Amazon sign $60B multi-generation AI chip deal*
   AWS committed to custom Qualcomm inference silicon plus 1.6 Tbit/s optical interconnects (via Qualcomm's Alphawave acquisition); Amazon got warrants on 25M Qualcomm shares (~$4B), 3.75M already vested.
   Why it matters: Qualcomm becomes AWS's first Western hyperscaler silicon partner, breaking Nvidia/Broadcom's grip on inference infrastructure.
   https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/qualcomm-amazon-data-center-infrastructure-deal.html

3. *OpenAI's Navier-Stokes "solve" turns into an authorship fight*
   10,000 OpenAI agents reportedly cracked the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize problem in 88 hours at multi-million-dollar compute cost — but NYU's Tristan Buckmaster says the effort followed rumors of his and Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge's near-solution, and disputes OpenAI's proposed authorship terms.
   Why it matters: sets an early precedent for how credit gets assigned when AI-agent swarms build on unpublished human research.
   https://www.axios.com/2026/09/08/openai-math-solution-navier-stokes-credit

4. *Solana's Transaction v1 goes live on mainnet today*
   The upgrade (SIMD-0296/0385) triples max transaction size from 1,232 to 4,096 bytes, natively supporting ZK proofs, BLS signatures, and large multisigs; legacy and v0 transactions keep working.
   Why it matters: unlocks more complex on-chain apps (confidential transfers, bigger multisig ops) without new transaction formats.
   https://solanacompass.com/news/solana-transaction-v1-heads-to-mainnet-september-9-format-breaking-changes-and-what-it-unlocks
